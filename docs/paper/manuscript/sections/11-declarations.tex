\sect{Declarations}\label{draft-declarations-and-author-review}

\textbf{Participants and ethics.} All data were recorded by the author on themself, using consumer wearables; no other people took part, and no clinical or third-party data were collected. Formal ethics review was not sought for this self-experimentation.

\textbf{Funding and competing interests.} This work was self-funded and received no external funding. The author declares no competing interests.

\textbf{Data availability.} The recordings were collected by the author on themself. Because they are physiological recordings of one identifiable person, they are not publicly released; the data and code are available from the author on reasonable request.

\textbf{Use of AI tools.} Claude (Anthropic) and ChatGPT (OpenAI) were used to assist with research, coding, and drafting and editing this manuscript. They did not generate any measurements or participant data. The author is responsible for the scientific content and its interpretation.
